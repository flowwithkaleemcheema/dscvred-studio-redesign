October 19, 2026

7 min read

Key Takeaways+
  • Webflow site plans cost $0 (Starter), $15/mo (Basic) or $25/mo (Premium), billed yearly.
  • The old CMS ($23) and Business ($39) plans no longer exist. Webflow merged them into Premium in May 2026.
  • Clutch reports most web design projects cost under $10,000, with an average of $38,105.
  • Webflow developers on Upwork typically charge $20 to $45 an hour.
  • A small Webflow site costs $1,584 in platform fees over 3 years, plus the build fee.
  • Compliance review, accessibility and integrations add the most cost for regulated firms.

A Webflow website costs $15 to $25 a month for the site plan, billed yearly, plus a one-time build fee. Build fees usually run from about $5,000 for a small agency-built site to $50,000 or more for a large custom build. Team seats and ongoing support add to the yearly total.

We're a Certified Webflow Partner that builds sites for fintech, finance, healthcare and B2B SaaS teams. This guide shows how much a Webflow website costs, where the money goes, and what changes for regulated firms. All plan prices come from Webflow's pricing page, checked October 2026.

How Much Does a Webflow Website Cost?

A Webflow website has two costs: the platform and the build. The platform is a monthly plan you pay Webflow. The build is what you pay a freelancer or agency to design and develop the site.

What does Webflow cost for a marketing website?

A typical B2B marketing site with a blog needs the Premium plan at $25/mo, billed yearly. Add a Core workspace at $19/mo for your team. The build is the larger cost: most 10 to 20 page marketing sites fall in the small or custom agency band above.

What's the cost of Webflow website design?

Design cost depends on how many page templates you need and whether you start from a template. A purchased template cuts design time. A custom design system costs more upfront but makes every new page faster to build later. Ask for design and development as separate lines in your quote.

Workspace plans and seats

Your team also needs a workspace plan. Core costs $19/mo and Growth costs $49/mo, billed yearly. Each workspace includes 1 full seat. Extra full seats cost $39/mo and limited seats for content editors cost $15/mo.

Add-ons

  • Localize for translated sites: from $9/mo
  • Analyze for site analytics: from $9/mo
  • Optimize for A/B testing and personalization: from $299/mo

Many articles still list a $23 CMS plan or a $39 Business plan. Those prices are out of date.

When a freelancer makes sense

A freelancer suits a simple site of 5 to 10 pages with a ready design and copy. You save money but take on project management, QA and launch yourself.

When an agency makes sense

An agency suits a site with a CMS, integrations or a review process. You pay more per hour but get strategy, design, development, QA, migration and training in one team. For regulated firms, an agency also plans for compliance review from the start.

Cost drivers for regulated firms

Financial services, healthcare and law firms pay for work most sites skip:

  • Compliance review rounds: disclosures and claims need sign-off before launch.
  • Accessibility: building and testing to WCAG standards takes extra design and QA time.
  • Secure forms: healthcare forms must not send patient data through Webflow's native forms, because Webflow isn't HIPAA compliant by default.
  • Reusable disclosures: a CMS field or component that shows the right disclaimer on every page.

A reusable disclosure field in Webflow CMS keeps the right disclaimer on every page.

HubSpot and CRM integration

Connecting Webflow forms to HubSpot is one of the most common add-ons. It covers form mapping, tracking code, lead routing and testing. Budget for it as its own line item in your quote.

What Does a Webflow Redesign Cost?

A Webflow redesign often costs about the same as a new build of the same size. Design and development are redone either way. A redesign costs less when you keep your content, page structure and CMS, and more when you migrate from another platform.

Migration adds three tasks:

  1. Moving content into Webflow CMS Collections
  2. Setting up 301 redirects from every old URL
  3. Checking SEO settings and forms after launch

Those tasks were part of our Wix to Webflow migration for Lightning Reach, which still went from kickoff to launch in 6 weeks.

Is Webflow Cheaper Than WordPress?

Webflow often costs more to build and less to run than WordPress. Webflow includes hosting, security updates and SSL in the plan. WordPress needs separate hosting, plugin updates and security work, which you or an agency must manage, one of the main differences between Webflow and WordPress.

What Should a Webflow Quote Include?

A complete Webflow quote lists every phase, so you can compare agencies line by line. Check that yours covers:

  1. Discovery and sitemap
  2. Design for each page template
  3. Webflow build, including CMS Collections and components
  4. Integrations, such as HubSpot forms and analytics
  5. Content migration and 301 redirects
  6. QA across devices and browsers
  7. Editor training and launch support
  8. Plan and seat costs you'll pay Webflow

If a quote leaves out migration, QA or training, ask why. Those gaps often turn into change requests later.

FAQ

How much does a 10-page Webflow website cost?

A 10-page Webflow site typically costs about $5,000 to $15,000 with an agency, or less with a freelancer. Add $25/mo for the Premium plan if it has a blog.

Do I pay separately for Webflow hosting?

No. Hosting is included in your Webflow site plan, along with SSL and a global CDN. Your domain is a separate yearly cost.

Can I build a Webflow website myself?

Yes. The Starter plan is free for learning. A professional result takes time to learn, so most businesses hire a freelancer or agency.

Why is Webflow more expensive than Wix or Squarespace?

Webflow gives complete design control, a structured CMS and clean code. Those suit custom marketing sites, and they take more skill to build.
